# TezFlow Round 1 Pitch

## 90-Second Pitch

Good morning.

Our challenge is disaster-resilient logistics. During floods in India, food, medicines, and other essential goods must reach affected communities quickly. But logistics teams often work with fragmented information. Weather updates, road conditions, inventory levels, and demand signals are spread across different systems. By the time a problem is visible, deliveries are delayed and critical supplies may already be running short.

Our primary user is the regional logistics control-room manager. This person coordinates warehouses, carriers, drivers, hospitals, relief workers, and government teams. They do not need another dashboard full of alerts. They need one trusted and explainable action plan that they can approve quickly.

That is why we propose TezFlow, an AI-enabled supply-chain control system. TezFlow combines weather, traffic, demand, route, and operational signals into one regional Chaos Score. It predicts where disruption is likely to spread, explains the impact in plain language, and recommends actions such as rerouting vehicles, transferring inventory, and prioritizing emergency deliveries.

The AI agent detects, predicts, explains, and recommends. The human manager remains accountable: they validate local conditions and approve high-impact decisions. Approved reroutes can then be sent to SAP through a secure backend integration, connecting intelligent recommendations with enterprise execution.

TezFlow creates value for government agencies, humanitarian organizations, healthcare networks, and logistics operators by reducing response time, preventing cascading shortages, improving coordination, and supporting fair access to essential supplies.

Our unique value is simple: TezFlow does not stop at tracking a disruption or generating an alert. It connects early prediction, explainable AI, human decision-making, multi-node coordination, and SAP-executable action in one workflow.

TezFlow turns supply-chain disruption from a delayed reaction into an explainable, equitable, and executable response.

Thank you.

## Slide Cues

1. **Persona:** Regional logistics control-room manager coordinating essential goods during floods.
2. **User needs:** Early warning, one shared view, explainable recommendations, fast coordination, and fair prioritization.
3. **Problem:** Fragmented information causes delayed rerouting, stockouts, outdated driver instructions, and cascading failures.
4. **Solution:** TezFlow fuses signals, calculates risk, recommends actions, synchronizes teams, and connects approved actions to SAP.
5. **Impact:** Faster response, fewer shortages, better resource use, reliable essential-goods delivery, and accountable decisions.

## Strong Closing Line

> TezFlow gives supply-chain teams the foresight to see disruption early and the coordination layer to act before it becomes a crisis.
