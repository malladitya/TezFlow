# TezFlow: 5-Slide Presentation

## Slide 1: Persona

**Scenario:** Delivering food, medicines, and essential goods during floods in India.

**Primary persona:** Regional logistics control-room manager

- Coordinates warehouses, carriers, drivers, hospitals, and relief teams
- Makes time-sensitive allocation and rerouting decisions
- Is accountable for delivery reliability and fair access to supplies

**Supporting users:** Warehouse managers, fleet operators, relief workers, and government coordinators.

## Slide 2: User Needs

The logistics manager needs to:
- Know which routes and regions are at risk before failure
- See weather, traffic, demand, inventory, and route data together
- Understand why an AI recommendation was made
- Coordinate teams through one shared operational view
- Balance urgency, cost, safety, resilience, and equity

**Empathy insight:** “I do not need another alert. I need a trusted action plan I can approve quickly.”

## Slide 3: Problem Statement

**During floods, supply-chain teams often react only after delays and shortages have already spread.**

Road closures, demand spikes, and fragmented updates make it difficult to decide which route, warehouse, or community should be prioritized. Existing tools separate tracking, planning, and communication, so decisions are slow, manual, and difficult to explain.

**Key failure modes:** unusable routes, medicine stockouts, delayed warehouse transfers, and drivers receiving outdated instructions.

## Slide 4: Solution

**TezFlow is an AI-powered supply-chain control system.**

It:
1. Combines live weather, traffic, demand, and route data.
2. Calculates a regional **Chaos Score** to identify rising risk.
3. Uses AI to generate operational briefs and response strategies.
4. Recommends rerouting, stock transfers, and emergency prioritization.
5. Synchronizes actions across HQ, warehouse, and driver dashboards.
6. Sends approved reroute actions to SAP through a secure backend API.

**Agent-human interaction:** AI detects, predicts, explains, and recommends. The human manager validates local context and approves high-impact actions before SAP execution.

## Slide 5: Impact & Key Takeaway

**Expected impact:**
- Earlier disruption detection
- Faster, coordinated response
- Fewer cascading delays and shortages
- Better use of fleet and inventory
- More reliable access to critical supplies

**Business value:** Government agencies, humanitarian organizations, healthcare networks, and logistics companies can use TezFlow through enterprise subscriptions, deployment services, and SAP integration support.

**What makes TezFlow unique:** It connects prediction, explainable AI, human accountability, multi-node coordination, and SAP-executable action in one workflow.

**Key takeaway:**

> TezFlow turns supply-chain disruption from a delayed reaction into an explainable, equitable, and executable response.
